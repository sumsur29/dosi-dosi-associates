The Problem

Sound familiar?

Most manufacturers we meet are doing everything "right" — and still struggling. The issue usually isn't capacity, market, or effort. It's flow.

01

Trying to keep every machine busy

Local efficiency feels productive — but it's quietly clogging your floor with the wrong work.

02

Starting work without full kit

Half-started jobs pile up everywhere. Nothing finishes when it should.

03

Too many priorities

If everything is urgent, nothing is. Teams firefight instead of finishing.

The symptoms we see almost everywhere:

  • Orders are there, but dispatch is slow
  • Too much WIP, yet deliveries are delayed
  • Teams are busy, but output isn't improving
  • Constant firefighting across departments
  • Cash constraint that won't go away
  • Not getting enough orders
This is not a capacity or market issue. It is a flow issue.
Our Approach

How we fix it — in four steps.

We use the Theory of Constraints (TOC) — but simplified, on the shop floor, with your team.

1

Identify the constraint

Find the one bottleneck that's secretly governing your entire output.

2

Align everything to it

Every team, every release, every priority — subordinated to the constraint.

3

Control work release

Stop premature starts. Reduce WIP. Watch on-time delivery climb.

4

Stabilize & improve

Lock in the gains. Build a system that runs without daily firefighting.

"We don't improve machines. We improve flow — and flow improves everything."
